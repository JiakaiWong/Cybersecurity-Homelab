# Defensive Security Intro Walkthrough  
*A practical exercise in detecting, investigating, and responding to attacks.*

---

## Introduction  
Defensive security is the practice of protecting systems and devices from attacks.  
Instead of breaking into systems like offensive security, defenders focus on monitoring, detecting suspicious behaviour, investigating alerts, and responding before any damage occurs.

### What is the main goal of defensive security?  
**Answer:** Detect and respond to attacks

---

## Task 3: Stop the Attack  
**Scenario:**  
Joe now knows who the attacker is and what they are trying to access. The next step is containment. In defensive security, containment is the immediate priority because it stops the attack while it’s happening.

Your job is to block the attacker’s IP address.

### Steps  
1. Review the available security actions  
2. Add the attacker’s IP address into the firewall rule box  
   - `32.122.195.63`  
3. Select **BLOCK**  
4. Press **Apply**
